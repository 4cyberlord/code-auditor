Authentication flow hardening
============================

Touch ID is deliberately out of scope for now. Keep the flow PIN-first, local,
and predictable before adding any biometric path.

Done in this pass:
- Auto-lock the workbench after inactivity.
- Auto-lock after the machine sleeps or the app resumes from a long idle gap.
- Keep active executions safe by not idle-locking in the middle of a running job.
- Tighten the unlock form so empty usernames and incomplete PINs never hit the
  server.
- Show clearer retry and lockout state from the server, including attempts left
  immediately after a failed unlock.
- Require a fresh unlock before changing sensitive settings such as provider
  keys, cloud publishing credentials, or account PIN changes.
